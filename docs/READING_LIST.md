# Short reading list

Primary sources used in this review, ordered for a first pass. All are free to read. URLs are those recorded in `data/evidence_table.csv` (retrieval date 2026-10-12). Open them in a browser to confirm the current version. Items marked "automated access blocked" could not be fetched by the research tools; read them in a browser, and see `docs/DOWNLOAD_LIST.md`.

## Start here (binding or conditionally binding testing text)

1. **Abu Dhabi DoH, Responsible AI Standard V1 (October 2025).** https://www.doh.gov.ae/-/media/Feature/Resources/Standards/2025/Responsible-AI-Standard-V1.ashx
2. **Dubai Health Authority, Artificial Intelligence in the Healthcare Policy (HISHD/PP-09).** https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/082022/Artificial%20Intelligence%20Policy20228457.pdf
3. **DIFC Data Protection Regulations, Consolidated Version No. 2 (Regulation 10).** https://assets.difc.com/v1/media/edge/images/dubaiintern0078-difcexperie96c5-production-3253/media/project/difcexperiences/difc/difcwebsite/documents/laws--regulations/data-protection-regulation.pdf and the Regulation 10 Accreditation and Certification Framework: https://assets.difc.com/v1/media/edge/images/dubaiintern0078-difcexperie96c5-production-3253/media/project/difcexperiences/difc/difcwebsite/documents/registrars-and-commissioners/regulation-10/difc-reg-10-accreditation-and-certification-framework.pdf
4. **EU AI Act, consolidated text as amended in 2026** (Articles 9, 15; read to about Article 38 only by the research tools). https://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/02024R1689-20260727 (the consolidated copy states it has no legal effect; the Official Journal texts are authentic). Amending regulation (EU) 2026/1744: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj/eng
5. **US OMB Memorandum M-25-21** (minimum practices for high impact AI). https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/M-25-21-Accelerating-Federal-Use-of-AI-through-Innovation-Governance-and-Public-Trust.pdf
6. **Canada, Directive on Automated Decision Making** (automated access blocked). https://www.tbs-sct.canada.ca/pol/doc-eng.aspx?id=32592
