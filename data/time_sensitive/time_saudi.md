# Saudi AI instruments: status check (retrieved 2026-10-12)

Research, not legal advice. Access: WebFetch and WebSearch only. Companion file: time_saudi.csv (14 rows).

## Headline findings
1. SDAIA's main site (sdaia.gov.sa, EN and AR) returned "Request Rejected" on every page and PDF tried, each retried once where the same URL was tried twice. Per the access rule I stopped there. So the 2025 AI Ethics Principles PDF, GenAI Guidelines for Government, AI Adoption Framework, the Risk Management Framework executive summary and the AI Bias guide were NOT read. Your "SDAIA's page calls it a draft" claim is unverified from here.
2. SDAIA's other official host dgp.sdaia.gov.sa served AI Ethics Principles **Version 1.0, September 2023** (EN and AR). This conflicts with our "v1 May 2025". Likely different editions; the 2025 one is unread.
3. NCA: the AI Cybersecurity document is **still a draft** (AICG-1:2026), consultation opened 5 July 2026, comments due 5 August 2026, listed Closed on the NCA consultations page (updated 20/08/2026). No final seen.
4. SDAIA National AI Risk Management Framework: primary (Saudi Press Agency, state news agency) says **launched 14 July 2026** as a guidance reference, not April 2026. Document itself unread.

## 2. NCA AI Cybersecurity Guidelines
- English PDF (cdn.nca.gov.sa ..._AI_Cybersecurity_Controls_EN.pdf): title "Draft of AI Cybersecurity Guidelines", code AICG-1:2026, Public/TLP:Clear. Text layer is **partial**: headings, TOC and annexes extract; the Executive Summary, Introduction, Scope (p.8) and guideline clauses do not. No issue date, no version history. Four domains: Governance, Defense, Resilience, Third-Party and Cloud. Not OCRed, not fetched by another route. Internal conflict: some page headers carry Arabic "مقيد - داخلي" (restricted) while cover says Public.
- Guidelines vs Controls: the English file NAME says "Controls"; its content and every NCA web page (EN and AR: "إرشادات") say Guidelines. "AICC" does not appear in the extracted English text. Whether the Arabic PDF cover says ضوابط/AICC is unresolved because the Arabic PDF fetch failed twice with a permission-timeout (not a site block).
- Advisory vs mandatory: not stated anywhere I could read. Pages say the purpose is to ensure implementation of AI cybersecurity requirements and promote best practices. "Advisory, not mandatory" is an inference; mark unverified.
- NCA regulatory-documents page 1 lists 8 documents, all 2022; no AI document. Pages 2 and 3 not seen.
- Arabic web pages (nca.gov.sa/ar/...) were read via the fetch tool, which returns text it processed; only titles are quoted.
- Document for the user: Arabic PDF https://cdn.nca.gov.sa/api/files/public/upload/eb6f2457-8b99-403b-b8b9-35352c6feca8_AI_Cybersecurity_Guidelines-_AR--.pdf (check cover title, code, date, any binding wording) and the full English PDF (scope p.8 and clauses pp.16-23 had no text through WebFetch; the user should read the PDF by hand).

## 3. SDAIA National AI Risk Management Framework
- SPA English N2633781 and Arabic N2633725, both 14 July 2026: SDAIA launched a national framework as a guidance reference (مرجعًا إرشاديًا). Four phases, seven principles, seven risk types; likelihood-impact matrix. No version number, no approval or in-force wording.
- SPA N2644802, 30 July 2026: briefing to 45 entities; guidance methodology; no status.
- The SPA Arabic item points to an Arabic executive summary PDF on sdaia.gov.sa: https://sdaia.gov.sa/ar/MediaCenter/KnowledgeCenter/ResearchLibrary/ExecutiveSummaryAr.pdf (blocked). User to download. The full framework text was not located.
- Conflict: our "April 2026" is not supported by any primary I read; primary gives 14 July 2026.
