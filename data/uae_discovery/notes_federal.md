# UAE federal coverage: notes (retrieval date 2026-10-12)

Research, not legal advice. Statements below describe only what retrieved primary text says. Fetch tool returns extracted or summarised text, so locators are tool-reported and page numbers are approximate unless stated.

## What was found (primary text actually read)
1. **No federal binding or technical standard for pre-deployment AI testing in government was found.** Nothing read says testing is compulsory for government AI.
2. **AI Adoption Guideline in Government Services** (Ministry of Cabinet Affairs, PMO; English copy hosted on moj.gov.ae; Arabic files on government.ae and ai.gov.ae). Closest federal government-specific text: Execution stage says to evaluate the model against acceptance criteria before deployment, document test results, and Operations stage says to monitor against acceptance criteria. It presents itself as a guideline (advisory wording), so rows are `guidance`. No version or date was visible. The Arabic excerpts confirm a train, test, evaluate, deploy, monitor lifecycle sentence but the acceptance-criteria sentence was not surfaced; pagination differs from the English copy. Possible version difference is unverified.
3. **AI Ethics: Principles & Guidelines** (UAE AI and Blockchain Council, Dec 2022). Non-binding by its own text (s1.1, s1.2). Contains the most testing-related content: bias testing (3.1.2.2), external audit for systems informing critical decisions (3.2.2.3), tested fallback plans and testing/verification processes (3.5.2.2, 3.5.2.3). All `guidance`.
4. **AI Ethics Self-Assessment Tool** (AI Office): voluntary, self-assessed, "will not be audited".
5. **AI Guide** (undated, Minister of State for AI Office): algorithm testing before field deployment; RegLab testing licences. Guidance.
6. **National Cyber Security Policy for AI** (Cyber Security Council; portal page updated 02 Jul 2026, PDF v1.1): portal summary (English and Arabic) mentions comprehensive testing and validation, and testing against adversarial attacks. Binding status and who it applies to are NOT known because the full PDF was blocked. Rows marked `ambiguous`.
7. **PDPL (Federal Decree-Law 45/2021)**, English and Arabic originals: Art 21 impact assessment (conditional on high-risk automated processing), Art 20 security testing process. **Art 2(2)(a)-(b) excludes government data and government entities** from the decree-law. Executive regulations are referred to in many articles but were not found.
8. **National AI Strategy 2031**: proposes a "UAI Seal of Approval" (p.20), in future/conditional language; no evidence in this text that it exists.
9. **UAE Charter (2024)**, English (u.ae, ai.gov.ae PDF, uaelegislation.gov.ae) and Arabic (u.ae/ar): 12 principles. Safety and Human Oversight only. No testing, evaluation, assessment, validation or audit wording. No rows added.
10. Read with no testing content: International Stance policy (Sept 2024), MOHAP smart health policy (18 May 2026), Agentic AI page on u.ae, data protection page on u.ae, digital procurement policy (MoF), ai.gov.ae home, Council page, AI policy index, Cabinet Resolution 13/2021 (conformity assessment fees, no AI).
