# Abu Dhabi coverage: notes (retrieval date 2026-10-12)

Access: WebSearch and WebFetch only. WebFetch returns extracted or summarised text, so locators below are as reported by the tool and should be checked against the raw PDF before citing. Secondary sources were used only to locate primaries.

## Found (primary, read)
- ADGM FSRA Supplementary Guidance, Digital Investment Management (VER02.061125). Only Abu Dhabi find with real testing content for AI: regular testing of the model, error detection, monitoring, possible third party audit (s4.7(b)-(d)); "algorithm" covers ML/LLM/GenAI. Sector specific (robo advice), guidance. 3 rows.
- ADGM ODP Guidance on DPR 2021 Part 4 (DPIA) and Part 2 (rights, s10 automated decision making), plus the ADGM automated decision making brochure (May 2025 per tool). Risk assessment and human review, plus "test and confirm design assumptions" (brochure p.4). Not model performance testing. 6 rows.
- Abu Dhabi Law No. 3 of 2024 (AI and Advanced Technology Council), Official Gazette Issue 1, 31 Jan 2024, Arabic original. Enabling law: sets standards (Art 4(2)), tracks policy performance (4(11)), entities must implement Council decisions (9(1)). No power or duty to test AI systems. 2 rows.
- DoH instruments located and listed (not re-verified, no rows): Policy on Use of AI in Healthcare v0.9 (30 Apr 2018); Responsible AI Standard V1 (DoH/ST/DDGO/RAI/V1/2025, effective Oct 2025, next revision Oct 2026); Responsible AI Risk Management Protocol V1 (Oct 2025, revision Oct 2028); Radiology and Medical Imaging Standard (AI only supervision/monitoring). For the DoH status agent: the Risk Management Protocol, per the tool, cites a "DoH AI Policy" and "DoH AI Standard" dated July 2025 and does not mention an "AI Policy Standard" of June 2025. Treat the June 2025 name as unconfirmed.
- Press level only (official issuer, but no rule text): ITC AViTOMS release (12 Nov 2025, phased AV test permits, real time monitoring, instant shutdown); DMT/SASC autonomous maritime release (26 Jun 2026, "Manual of Requirements and Controls for Testing Autonomous Marine Crafts", Administrative Decision No. 17 of 2026). Both concern autonomy testing, not AI model evaluation, and the underlying texts were not read.

## Not found (re-checked, English and Arabic)
- Abu Dhabi government wide AI policy or standard with testing content: not found. DGE (which absorbed ADDA; adda.gov.ae redirects to dge.gov.ae) pages, its laws/resolutions/circulars index, and the Digital Strategy 2025-2027 releases (30 Sep 2025, English and Arabic) mention responsible and ethical AI frameworks, Chief Data and AI Officers and human oversight, but no testing, evaluation or assurance requirement. Absence here means not found by this search, not that none exists.
- Abu Dhabi Government Data Management Policy, Information Security Standards, AI ethics guidelines: not located. Only DoH ADHICS (healthcare infosec, not opened) surfaced.
- ADGM AI specific guidance beyond DPIA/ADM: none found. The ODP guidance index lists no AI titled document (title level check). Law-firm hits mention nothing primary.
- ADEO, TAMM, ADJD, Abu Dhabi Customs: no AI testing instrument. Only news. ADJD AI judicial platform coverage is news only.
- TII/ATRC: no official AI safety or evaluation document; only news and an insight article (not a standard).
- UAE federal "national AI test and validation lab" appeared in results (WAM, TradeArabia); it is federal, not Abu Dhabi, and was not opened.
