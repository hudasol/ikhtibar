# Dubai coverage re-check: notes (retrieval date 2026-10-12)

Scope: primary Dubai sources on testing, evaluation, assurance, audit, validation or conformity of AI systems. Method: WebSearch and WebFetch only, English and Arabic. Not legal advice. Every statement below is limited to what a retrieved primary page said. The fetch tool returns summaries or extracted text, so locators, page numbers and quotes should be spot-checked against the PDFs (the verification step should do this).

Files: search_log_dubai.csv (94 entries: 39 searches, 55 fetches), sources_dubai.csv (61 sources, including list-only items), newrows_dubai.csv (30 rows).

## 1. Found (primary text read)

1. Dubai AI Ethics Guidelines (Smart Dubai, v1.11, 30/12/2018), English official PDF, plus an Arabic edition. Non-binding (p.14 English). Contains testing-related guidance: data exploration and/or testing for bias (1.1.1.1), impact assessments (1.1.3.1), internal risk assessments (1.2.2.3), performance monitoring with vendors (1.2.2.7), quality checks (1.2.2.8). Guideline 1.2.3 (external audit of AI systems informing critical decisions) is suspended "until further notice" because no external auditing mechanism has been established (English p.24; Arabic edition p.22).
2. AI System Ethics Self-Assessment Tool page (digitaldubai.ae/self-assessment). Beta, English. Self-assessment only: the page says the tool will not be audited, checked or regulated. Guidelines are recommended, not mandatory. This resolves the earlier "toolkit not found" result: the working tool page is /self-assessment, not the /initiatives/ pages (those still return 404).
3. Digital Dubai consultation page (digitaldubai.ae/ai-ethics, closed 21 Nov 2019): says the Executive Council advised that all public entities implementing AI should use the toolkit. Lists "algorithmic audit tools" only as options under consultation.
4. Official newsroom and entity items on toolkit adoption: RTA reported to have made the toolkit mandatory for its own AI systems (Smart Dubai newsroom, 2 Feb 2020; secondary to RTA's own instrument, which was not located); DEWA applied it to 13 use cases with a minimum threshold (DEWA news, 18 Nov 2020).
5. Dubai Health Authority, Artificial Intelligence in the Healthcare (Policy), HISHD/PP-09 (strongest Dubai find). Mandatory for stated stakeholders (s4.11.1) with sanctions (s4.11.2). Requires independent third-party validation of all healthcare AI solutions (s4.3.3), regular independent third-party audits of AI functionality by end users (s4.8.5), required certification of AI software (s4.6.10), clinical performance monitoring (s4.6.11), auditable validation statements (s4.6.12), and DHA audit and inspection (s4.10.2). Caveats: issue date 01/07/2021; effective date is inconsistent in the document (01/10/2021 vs 1/7/2021); stated revision date 01/07/2026 has passed, so a newer version may exist (none found); the policy does not define validation or name a validator or certification scheme. Circular CIR-2021-00000141 (22 Aug 2021) transmitted it to DHA licensees "for adherence and compliance". This is a sector instrument, not a government-wide one.
6. AI Integration Matrix Framework for Government Organizations (Dubai Digital Authority whitepaper v1.0, Aug 2025; press release 28 Apr 2026). Self-described as a strategic framework, not a formal policy (p.5). s10.3 says AI initiatives under DDA's domain are required to show ethical alignment and undergo validation; s10.2 describes QA testing cycles. Scope is DDA's own initiatives. Row status recorded as ambiguous. Press releases (English and Arabic) contain no testing provisions.
7. Information Security Regulation v2.0 (DESC): generic security testing, vulnerability test and sign-off controls for Dubai Government Entities (6.1.6.3, 8.6.1, 8.7.2). Technology neutral; no AI mention in the visible text. Rows are marked ambiguous because application to AI is an inference.
8. Law No. (9) of 2023 on autonomous vehicles: RTA technical inspection and licensing, and RTA approval of changes to the Autonomous Driving System. Adjacent to AI; the law mentions AI only in its objectives.
9. Entity AI management policies aligned to ISO/IEC 42001: Dubai Department of Finance (Arabic, read in Arabic) and Dubai Culture (bilingual). Commitments on risk assessment and internal audit of the management system. No testing or validation of AI systems. Binding effect not stated.
10. Context items read, with no testing provisions: Dubai Universal Blueprint summary pages (u.ae) and launch article (protocol.dubai.ae); Dubai State of AI Report (Digital Dubai and DFF; testing and impact assessment content describes EU, US, Canada and Singapore, not Dubai); Law 24/2023 (Dubai Data and Statistics Establishment; Art. 6 gives it AI standards, frameworks and policy-proposal mandates but no testing obligation); Law 26/2015 (Dubai Data Law; no AI mention); Executive Council Resolution 34/2021 (Oyoon; committee to develop AI policies, no testing); Dubai AI Seal pages (dub.ai English and Arabic, Media Office); Dubai Finance item on AI Seal suppliers; DESC launch news item; Dubai Cyber Security Strategy 2023; Dubai Police ISO/IEC 42001 certification news; DFF taskforce news; Dubai Customs press item; federal AI and Data Authority news (14 Jun 2026).
11. Dubai AI Seal. Per dub.ai it classifies AI companies into tiers S to E from activities, projects and economic contribution. It does not test AI systems. Dubai Finance (19 Oct 2025) called on government entities to contract Seal holders. Relevant to supplier certification, not system testing.

## 6. Conflicts and cautions to carry into the merge

- Toolkit status: self-assessment page (recommended, not audited) versus consultation page (Executive Council advised use by all public entities) versus RTA mandatory use for its own systems (newsroom report). Rows marked ambiguous or flagged.
- Guidelines English versus Arabic edition: the Arabic edition (as rendered by the fetch tool) uses "يجب" (rendered must/should) where the English says "should consider" for 1.2.2.3 and 1.2.2.7; page numbers differ (1.2.3 at p.22 Arabic, p.24 English). The Arabic edition's own wording could not be compared line by line; confidence on modal strength is low. The first tool pass on the Arabic PDF called it a bilingual, incomplete extraction; the second said Arabic only. Treated as Arabic.
- Dubai Finance AI Seal item: English says "urged" and "instruction", Arabic says "دعت" (called on) and "اشتراط" (requirement); English says "six key areas", Arabic says six categories. Neither names a circular. Not a testing provision.
- AI Integration Matrix: PDF says Aug 2025 v1.0, press release dated 28 Apr 2026; scope is DDA, press framing is government-wide.
- DHA policy: effective date inconsistent inside the document; revision date passed.
- ISR and the autonomous vehicle law: generic or adjacent to AI; AI applicability is an inference.
- Dubai Culture and Dubai Finance policies are internal commitments; no binding language read.
