# Related work collection notes

All 61 listed works were opened with WebFetch (landing page, abstract page or PDF first page) before listing. The `read_level` column is `abstract_page` where an abstract or summary was visible, and `landing_page_only` where the page gave only metadata or a teaser (Stanford AI Index 2025, OECD.AI policy database, OECD.AI UAE page, NIST AI RMF page, Artstein and Poesio 2008, Krippendorff 2011). No work was read in full text. Fields not shown on the page are left empty (for example year for Ojewale et al., Hughes, Rao and Callison-Burch BibTeX paper, Naser, and Perez et al. SLR kappa; authors for Krippendorff 2011, Scientific Reports citation paper, OECD and Stanford pages).

Session constraint: the WebSearch budget (200 calls per turn, shared) ran out part way through, and WebFetch only accepts URLs that appeared in search results (direct arXiv guesses were refused). So searches for NIST AI 600-1, UAE and Saudi governance literature, GCC comparisons and Arabic AI policy returned nothing and were not rerun.
