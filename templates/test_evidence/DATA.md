# DATA: Data and training corpus quality validation

> PROPOSAL. This template was written by the maintainer from the retrieved sources. It is not a requirement of any authority and no authority has reviewed it. Adapt it to your entity and tier.

## What to run

- Check data for accuracy, completeness, duplicates, labelling quality and provenance.
- Check lawful basis and permission to use the data for this purpose.
- Check that test data is separate from training data and reflects the deployment setting.

## What to record

- Data sources, dates, versions, sampling and labelling procedure, and quality checks with results.
- Known gaps and their expected effect.

## What a reviewer looks for

- Label quality was sampled and measured.
- Provenance can be traced for the data that matters most.
