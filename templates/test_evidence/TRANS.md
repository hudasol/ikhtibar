# TRANS: Transparency, explainability and reproducibility checks

> PROPOSAL. This template was written by the maintainer from the retrieved sources. It is not a requirement of any authority and no authority has reviewed it. Adapt it to your entity and tier.

## What to run

- Check that explanations or reason codes shown to users match what the system actually used.
- Check that disclosures and labels appear where they should and survive export or copy.
- Re run a saved test and confirm the same result from the same version and data.

## What to record

- Explanation method, user testing results, disclosure wording and placement.
- Version identifiers, data snapshots and commands needed to reproduce results.

## What a reviewer looks for

- Explanations are faithful, not decorative.
- Another team can reproduce the headline results from the saved record.
