# MON: Post deployment monitoring and re assessment

> PROPOSAL. This template was written by the maintainer from the retrieved sources. It is not a requirement of any authority and no authority has reviewed it. Adapt it to your entity and tier.

## What to run

- Monitor performance, drift, outcomes, complaints and overrides on a schedule that matches the stakes.
- {'Define triggers for re testing': 'model or data change, vendor update, scope change, incident.'}
- Rehearse incident escalation, suspension and rollback.

## What to record

- Metrics, thresholds, dashboards, review dates and owners.
- Incidents, decisions taken and lessons learned.

## What a reviewer looks for

- Alerts have owners and a response time.
- Rollback has been practised, not only written.
