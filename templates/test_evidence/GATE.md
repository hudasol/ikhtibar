# GATE: Acceptance criteria and release decision

> PROPOSAL. This template was written by the maintainer from the retrieved sources. It is not a requirement of any authority and no authority has reviewed it. Adapt it to your entity and tier.

## What to run

- Write acceptance criteria before testing, mapped to the risk tier.
- Hold a release decision at an authority level matched to the tier, using the evidence pack.
- Record conditions, waivers and the rule for unacceptable residual risk.

## What to record

- Criteria, evidence reference for each, decision, decision maker, date and conditions.

## What a reviewer looks for

- Waivers are explicit and time limited.
- The decision maker saw the weak results as well as the strong ones.
