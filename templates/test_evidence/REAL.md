# REAL: Real world, sandbox and pilot testing

> PROPOSAL. This template was written by the maintainer from the retrieved sources. It is not a requirement of any authority and no authority has reviewed it. Adapt it to your entity and tier.

## What to run

- Run a limited pilot with defined users, duration, stop conditions and monitoring.
- Agree in advance with the responsible authority what the pilot may and may not do, where such agreement is required.
- Compare pilot results with the pre deployment tests.

## What to record

- Pilot plan, participants, consent and safeguards, stop criteria, and results.
- Differences between pilot and test environment results.

## What a reviewer looks for

- Stop conditions were real and were tested.
- Harm to participants was monitored.
