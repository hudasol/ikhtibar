# CONF: Conformity assessment, independent review, audit or certification

> PROPOSAL. This template was written by the maintainer from the retrieved sources. It is not a requirement of any authority and no authority has reviewed it. Adapt it to your entity and tier.

## What to run

- Identify which instrument requires or recommends independent review for this entity and tier (this is often UNKNOWN until legal review).
- Give the reviewer the evidence pack from the other tests, and the system access they need.
- Record findings and how each was closed.

## What to record

- Reviewer identity, qualifications and independence, scope, access given, dates.
- Findings, closure evidence, and the final opinion or certificate with its conditions.

## What a reviewer looks for

- Independence from the development team.
- The scope matches the system as deployed, including vendor components.
