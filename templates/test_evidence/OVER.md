# OVER: Human oversight validation

> PROPOSAL. This template was written by the maintainer from the retrieved sources. It is not a requirement of any authority and no authority has reviewed it. Adapt it to your entity and tier.

## What to run

- Trial the review and override process with real reviewers under realistic workload and time pressure.
- Measure how often reviewers accept, change or reject outputs, and whether wrong outputs are caught.
- Test that an override or stop actually takes effect.

## What to record

- Reviewer roles, training, workload assumptions, and acceptance and override rates.
- Cases where wrong outputs were missed and why.
- Evidence that stop, override and escalation work.

## What a reviewer looks for

- Evidence that reviewers can disagree with the system in practice (automation bias is addressed).
- Reviewers have authority, competence and time.
