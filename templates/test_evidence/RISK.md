# RISK: Risk, impact and compliance assessment

> PROPOSAL. This template was written by the maintainer from the retrieved sources. It is not a requirement of any authority and no authority has reviewed it. Adapt it to your entity and tier.

## What to run

- Identify affected people and groups, harms, likelihood and severity, and legal obligations that apply to the entity.
- Assign an impact or risk tier and the controls that follow from it.
- Review the assessment when the system, data or use changes.

## What to record

- Tier, reasoning, owners, controls, residual risk and approver.
- Applicable instruments checked and which could not be checked (unknown).

## What a reviewer looks for

- Unknowns are written down with an owner and a date.
- The tier decides how deep later tests go.
